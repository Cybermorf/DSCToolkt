Active Forces

The Dynamic Influence Engine treats major antagonists, factions, cults, or disasters as active forces. Each force has a Public Face, Primary Goal, Political / Corruptive / Disruptive Influence (0–6), a six-segment Agenda Clock, Awareness of the party (0–4), and Pressure (0–4). Use Force Turn after major operations, downtime, or journeys.
